1. Form, Denomination and Title

The EUR 400,000,000 1.25 per cent. Convertible Bonds due 2031 (the "Bonds") of Exemple SA (the
"Issuer") are issued in bearer form in the denomination of EUR 100,000 each. The Bonds are issued
at 100 per cent. of their principal amount on 15 January 2026 (the "Issue Date").

2. Status

The Bonds constitute direct, unconditional, unsubordinated and unsecured obligations of the Issuer
and rank pari passu among themselves and with all other unsecured obligations of the Issuer.

3. Interest

The Bonds bear interest from and including the Issue Date at the rate of 1.25 per cent. per annum,
payable semi-annually in arrear on 15 January and 15 July in each year, commencing on 15 July 2026.

4. Conversion Right

Each Bondholder has the right to convert each Bond into ordinary shares of the Issuer at any time
from the Issue Date until the seventh business day before the Maturity Date. The initial
conversion price is EUR 54.00 per share (the "Conversion Price"), corresponding to an initial
conversion ratio of 1,851.8519 shares per Bond. The Conversion Price represents a premium of
35 per cent. above the reference share price of the Issuer on the pricing date.

5. Dividend Protection

The Conversion Price will be adjusted for any dividend paid by the Issuer in excess of EUR 0.80
per share in any financial year.

6. Redemption at Maturity

Unless previously redeemed, converted or purchased and cancelled, the Bonds will be redeemed at
their principal amount on 15 January 2031 (the "Maturity Date").

7. Redemption at the Option of the Issuer

On or after 5 February 2029, the Issuer may redeem all, but not some only, of the Bonds at their
principal amount together with accrued interest, provided that the volume weighted average price
of the shares on at least 20 trading days during a period of 30 consecutive trading days exceeds
130 per cent. of the Conversion Price in effect on each such trading day (the "Soft Call").
The Issuer may also redeem all outstanding Bonds at any time if less than 15 per cent. of the
Bonds originally issued remain outstanding (clean-up call).

8. Redemption at the Option of Bondholders

Each Bondholder may require the Issuer to redeem its Bonds at their principal amount together with
accrued interest on 15 January 2029 (the "Put Date").

9. Change of Control

Following a Change of Control, each Bondholder may require the Issuer to redeem its Bonds at their
principal amount together with accrued interest, and the Conversion Price will be temporarily
reduced in accordance with a ratchet formula.

10. Governing Law

The Bonds are governed by French law.
